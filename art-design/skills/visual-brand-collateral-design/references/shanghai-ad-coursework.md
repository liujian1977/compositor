# Shanghai Open University Advertising Design Coursework Notes

Use when a user is preparing the 上海开放大学《广告设计》专科作业 / 期末作品 based on the 2025–2026 second-semester documents.

## Assessment Structure

- Course: 《广告设计》
- Overall score: 形成性作业 50% + 作品考 50% = 100%
- Deadline clue: before week 15

## Formative Assignments

Four assignments should revolve around one shared theme. Materials and dimensions may be customized. Each assignment should submit:

- Original editable production file
- JPG export

Required formats:

1. Newspaper advertisement / 报纸广告
2. Magazine advertisement / 杂志广告
3. Poster design / 招贴设计
4. POP advertisement / POP广告

## Final Work Exam

Theme: “魅力上海·城市印象”

Design an A4 poster around Shanghai's city image. The work may choose one or combine several directions:

1. Traditional and modern fusion, e.g. Shikumen vs. Lujiazui
2. City spirit: openness, innovation, inclusiveness
3. Cultural symbols: the Bund, Yu Garden, lane culture, film festival, etc.
4. Sustainable Shanghai: environmental protection, smart city, future vision

Submission:

- A4 size
- 200 dpi (source may say “200dip”; interpret as dpi)
- Original production file + JPG
- AI assistance is allowed, but the work must be the student's own creation

A4 at 200 dpi:

- Portrait: about 1654 × 2339 px
- Landscape: about 2339 × 1654 px

## Scoring Rubric

- Design creativity: 20% — clear theme, original, novel
- Layout design: 20% — complete style, aesthetic, standardized and rigorous
- Color/style application: 20% — reasonable color design, memorable visual impression
- Overall effect: 40% — beautiful overall presentation

Note: if the work is found not to be personally created, score is 0.

## Recommended Campaign Strategy

Use one shared campaign concept across the four formative works and final poster. Strong default:

“传统与现代交融的上海 / Shanghai where tradition meets modernity”

Useful recurring elements:

- Shikumen / 石库门
- Lane culture / 弄堂
- The Bund / 外滩
- Lujiazui skyline / 陆家嘴
- Oriental Pearl Tower / 东方明珠
- Shanghai Tower / 上海中心
- Huangpu River / 黄浦江
- Haipai culture / 海派文化
- Modern urban light and reflections

Recommended styles:

- 新中式插画
- 几何水墨融合
- 半抽象油画

Campaign adaptation:

- Newspaper ad: clearer information hierarchy; can use lower saturation or limited-color treatment
- Magazine ad: more refined, artistic, image-led visual
- Poster: strongest key visual and theme statement
- POP ad: adapt into display stand, subway/mall sign, counter card, or offline promotional display

## Authorship Evidence

Because the exam warns that non-self-created work scores 0, preserve:

- Prompt drafts and final prompts if using AI
- Sketches/thumbnails
- Intermediate exports
- Editable source files/layers
- Notes explaining manual edits and design decisions
